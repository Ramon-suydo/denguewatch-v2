# Health-worker response protocol — review draft

**Purpose:** manually triage a rise in delayed, unverified community aggregates.
This is an awareness workflow, not a diagnostic or outbreak declaration protocol.
A qualified health-office owner must approve it before operational use.

1. **Check context.** Record time, source/as-of date, area/onset-week window and
   whether data is available. No published group does not mean zero illness.
   Distinguish official historical counts from community reports. Historical
   model thresholds cannot establish today's risk.
2. **Review privately.** An appointed moderator checks pending/quarantined
   reports for duplicates, bursts, accidental identifying text and implausible
   dates. Do not export raw records to chats, personal email or shared documents.
   Approval is content moderation, not medical verification.
3. **Seek corroboration.** Compare the signal with current authorized health-office
   surveillance and reporting conditions. Document gaps and alternative
   explanations. This app's official sources end in 2023 (NCR comparison January
   2021), so current corroboration must come from the responsible health office.
4. **Act through existing health procedures.** Share verified prevention guidance
   and arrange qualified review/consultation as appropriate. Do not diagnose,
   declare an outbreak, order fogging or publish a small group's identity from
   app counts alone. Urgent warning signs require clinical care independently
   of any app signal; follow DOH/local clinical procedures.
5. **Record and follow up.** Keep a restricted record of reviewer, aggregate
   window, corroboration source, decision/reason and follow-up date. Use IDs,
   not raw notes or withdrawal codes. Escalate suspected privacy/security
   incidents to the appointed owner/DPO and preserve access/audit evidence.

Reference: [DOH 4S guidance](https://ro7.doh.gov.ph/news-and-advisories/98-press-releases/2514-doh-central-visayas-chd-underscores-the-significance-of-4s-in-dengue-mitigation).
No numerical community-spike threshold has been validated; evaluation is future
work. This protocol does not claim effectiveness from a usability or field study.
