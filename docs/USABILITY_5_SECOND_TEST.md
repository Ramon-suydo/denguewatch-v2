# Five-second usability test: owner-run protocol

Status: NEEDS OWNER. No human sessions have been conducted or invented. Automated structure/axe checks do not establish comprehension.

Recruit first-time English and Filipino readers, including a screen-reader user. Explain that this is a historical research prototype. Do not train them on the terminology before the test. Use the same saved example week and device dimensions for comparability. Obtain appropriate institutional consent for any recordings; do not collect health disclosures.

1. Show Dashboard for five seconds, then hide it.
2. Ask: What is this page for? Which three areas had higher estimates? What week was shown? Was this a current forecast? What action would you take?
3. Reopen it. Ask the reader to open Risk Map, find Manila, change weeks and describe predicted chance versus the recorded outcome.
4. Ask why Live is disabled and how newer data would be supplied.
5. Switch Filipino. Repeat the key mode/date/action tasks and record misunderstood terms.
6. With a keyboard/screen reader, reach every city, use the table, read the mode/date and find warning signs/privacy.

Do not count guessing as successful understanding. Record exact participant wording without names or health details. Review failures before claiming the five-second goal passes.
