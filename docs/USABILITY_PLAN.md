# Usability plan — results intentionally blank

Recruit consenting adult volunteers who resemble public users and at least one
staff reviewer if available. Do not collect names, health histories, real reports,
tracking codes or staff passwords as research observations. Use participant IDs.
Explain that this is a historical research prototype, not medical advice. Obtain
school/ethics/DPO approval appropriate to the study before recruitment.

## Five task scenarios

1. **Understand status:** from Home, state whether the app can establish risk
   today, identify the last source date, and find prevention guidance.
2. **Read uncertainty:** find Manila's one/two-week historical forecast, explain
   the 80% interval, change M to Z, and describe the absent-date assumption.
3. **Compare history:** open Metro Manila, select a historical week/year, find
   Manila's recorded rate and explain the January 2021 banner/2020 denominator.
4. **Consider reporting safely:** read the notice, identify what would be
   collected, explain that collection is closed, and find withdrawal instructions.
   Never submit a real person's health information during usability testing.
5. **Assess evidence:** find the weather comparison and a COVID/year fold,
   explain whether weather improved the matched forecast, then show a keyboard
   path to the data-table alternative. Staff participants may separately inspect
   an authorized local empty moderation queue and CSV preview; no hosted writes.

Record completion (independent/assisted/not completed), time, critical
misunderstandings, keyboard barriers, observed behavior and optional participant
quotes with consent. Do not coach until a task is stopped; distinguish moderator
help from independent success. Include a 360px phone, keyboard and reduced-motion
pass. Note device/browser/assistive technology without device fingerprinting.

## SUS questionnaire and response sheet

Administer the **unaltered ten-item System Usability Scale** after the tasks,
using [Brooke's original questionnaire and scoring source](https://hci-studies.org/methods-and-measures/downloads/SUS_Brooke1996.pdf).
Save/print that source before an offline session; keep its attribution. The
labels below are item references for recording responses, not replacement
wording or a modified validated questionnaire. Each response is 1–5; do not fill
unanswered items by guessing. Obtain an appropriate validated language version
before administering in a different language.

For standard SUS scoring, transform odd items as response−1 and even items as
5−response, sum, then multiply by 2.5. The 0–100 score is not a percentage of
successful tasks. Report sample size, missing responses, mean/spread and context;
a small convenience sample does not establish general usability.
